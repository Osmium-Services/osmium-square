**Square** takes card payments on your own checkout page. The card form stays on your site, and customers are never sent to Square.

## Set it up

1. In the Square Developer Console, find the **Application ID** and **Access Token** on your application's Credentials page, and the **Location ID** under Locations.
2. Open **Square Settings** and enter them. Sandbox and Production values are separate and are not interchangeable.
3. Tick **Enable Square**.
4. Set **Square Mode** to **Sandbox** while testing, then **Production**.
5. Press **Save Settings**.
6. In **Settings > Payments**, choose Square as the **Active Checkout Provider**.

**Accepted payment types** are shown as payment badges on the site.
